- **Milestone 1**
  - Created CAD models for the robot arm, servo mounting plate, and assembly components using Autodesk Fusion 360
  - Taught Camille and Crystal the fundamentals of CAD modeling and assembly design in Autodesk Fusion 360
  - Collaborated with team members to brainstorm the robot’s overall mechanical design and competition strategy
  - Presented the “Pivoting Arm” and “Additional Modifications” slides during the milestone presentation

- **Milestone 2**
  - Mechanically mounted the DC motor and wheel assembly to the robot chassis
  - Assisted in debugging and reorganizing the robot’s electrical wiring to improve reliability and accessibility

- **Milestone 3**
  - Mounted the QTI and color sensors to the underside of the robot chassis for line detection and color sensing
  - Wired and integrated the QTI and color sensors with the Arduino control system
  - Developed the counting algorithm enabling the robot to detect and stop at the black border
  - Assisted in debugging electrical connections and testing the robot code during integration and performance trials

- **Milestone 4**
  - Finalized arm CAD and submitted files to the RPL for 3D printing
  - Mounted the completed arm assemblies to the robot chassis
  - Wired and integrated the servo motors with the robot’s electrical system
  - Designed and installed a rear support board to reduce outward bending of the arm mounting structure and improve stability

  - Edited and refined CAD figures and renderings included in the final report
